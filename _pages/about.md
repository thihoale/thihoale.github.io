I am **Thi Hoa LE**, a PhD student in Computer Science at
**Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1**, conducting my research
at **LIRIS** under the supervision of
**Prof. Angela Bonifati** and **Dr. Andrea Mauri**.

My research focuses on **data management and data quality**,
particularly user-guided data curation in distributed
environments. I am also interested in **Large Language Models
(LLMs), AI Agents, Natural Language Processing (NLP)**,
and **Human-in-the-Loop approaches**.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a Master's degree in
**Data and Information Systems Science (DISS)** at
Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 and undertook a research
internship at LIRIS. Previously, I studied at the
**Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology
(PTIT)** in Hanoi, Vietnam, and worked as a
**Data Scientist in the Optimization Team at VCCorp**.

## Research Interests

- Data Management and Data Quality
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI Agents and Agentic Systems
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Human-in-the-Loop Data Curation
- Distributed Data Management
