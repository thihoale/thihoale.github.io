**Authors:** Hoa Thi Le, Angela Bonifati, Andrea Mauri

### Overview

This talk presents an exploratory study on using Large
Language Models (LLMs) to generate consistency rules
for property graphs.

The study investigates the ability of LLMs to identify
schema-based constraints, including primary keys,
attribute uniqueness, and label enforcement, as well
as more complex consistency patterns.
